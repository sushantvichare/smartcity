# Citizen Complaint Categories

## Purpose

This document describes common categories for citizen complaints in a
Smart City application.

## Common Categories

### Roads and Potholes

Complaints involving:

-   Potholes
-   Damaged roads
-   Road surface problems
-   Unsafe road conditions

### Street Lighting

Complaints involving:

-   Non-functional street lights
-   Poor lighting
-   Damaged lighting infrastructure

### Garbage and Waste

Complaints involving:

-   Missed collection
-   Garbage accumulation
-   Overflowing bins
-   Illegal dumping

### Water Supply

Complaints involving:

-   Water supply interruptions
-   Leakage
-   Low pressure
-   Water quality concerns

### Drainage

Complaints involving:

-   Blocked drains
-   Waterlogging
-   Drain damage
-   Flooding-related infrastructure issues

### Traffic and Signals

Complaints involving:

-   Traffic signals
-   Road congestion
-   Parking problems
-   Traffic-management concerns

### Public Health and Sanitation

Complaints involving:

-   Sanitation
-   Public cleanliness
-   Pest-related concerns
-   Other civic health issues

## Classification

Complaint classification should be based on the categories available in
the actual dataset.

If a complaint does not clearly fit a category, the assistant should
identify it as ambiguous rather than forcing an unsupported
classification.

## Prediction

If the complaint model predicts complaint volume or citizen
satisfaction, the assistant should clearly label the value as a model
prediction.
