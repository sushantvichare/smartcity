# Traffic Patterns

## General Patterns

Traffic demand can vary by:

-   Time of day
-   Day of week
-   Location
-   Weather
-   Events
-   Road characteristics
-   Vehicle volume
-   Traffic incidents

The AI assistant should prefer the project's historical dataset when
discussing patterns for specific Mumbai locations.

## Time of Day

Commuter demand can create recurring traffic peaks during morning and
evening periods.

For this project, the exact peak periods should be learned from the
traffic dataset rather than assumed to be identical for every road.

## Day of Week

Weekday and weekend traffic patterns may differ.

The assistant should compare historical observations before making a
claim about a particular day.

## Weather

Rain and other weather conditions can affect traffic by changing driving
behavior, road conditions, visibility, and travel demand.

The chatbot should only attribute a prediction to weather when weather
is included in the model input or supporting data.

## Events

Large public events can temporarily increase traffic demand around event
locations.

The assistant should use recorded event information when available and
should not invent events.

## Location

Different areas and road segments can have substantially different
traffic patterns.

For location-specific questions, prioritize:

1.  Model prediction.
2.  Historical traffic data.
3.  Retrieved traffic documentation.

## RAG Response Rule

When explaining a traffic prediction, distinguish:

-   **Prediction:** generated by the ML model.
-   **Historical pattern:** derived from project data.
-   **General knowledge:** retrieved from this knowledge base.

This prevents the assistant from presenting general traffic knowledge as
if it were a measured observation.
